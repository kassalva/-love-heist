My Stoopi,

It's crazy to think that our story started back in high school. Before the trips, before the wedding, before all the memories we've made together, there were just two teenagers who had no idea how much life they were going to experience together.

And now, here we are.

Ten years married and almost seventeen years of choosing each other.

I want you to know how grateful I am for you. I admire your ambition and your work ethic more than I probably tell you. I see how hard you work and how much you want to build a good life for us, and I hope you know that I notice it. I appreciate every sacrifice, every long day, and every time you've pushed yourself because you wanted to give us the best life you could.

But one of the things I love most about you is that somehow, even after all these years, you can still make me laugh. You have a way of making ordinary moments fun, and some of my favorite memories are the ones where we're just being ourselves and laughing at something that probably wouldn't make anyone else laugh.

Thank you for your patience with me. Thank you for your compassion. Thank you for loving me through every version of myself and for continuing to choose me.

We've been so lucky to see pieces of the world together—Rome, Paris, Madrid, Barcelona, Japan, Mexico, Hawaii, and all the places in between. But when I look back at those trips, I realize that what made them special wasn't necessarily the place. It was who I was experiencing them with.

I can't wait to see where life takes us next. I want more trips with you, more adventures, more ridiculous stories, more pictures, more places we've never seen, and more memories we haven't made yet.

And maybe, someday, another little person or two who gets to call us Mom and Dad. ❤️

Whatever our future looks like, I just hope I'm standing next to you for all of it.

If I could go back to that high school hallway and tell that younger version of us everything that was coming—our wedding, our travels, our laughter, our struggles, and all these years together—I don't think we would have believed it.

But I wouldn't change a thing.

Thank you for being my husband, my travel partner, my favorite person to laugh with, and the person I want beside me for the rest of my life.

You may have started this heist back in 2009, Stoopi…

but after all these years, you've officially gotten away with my heart.

And honestly?

I don't want it back.

Here's to our first ten years of marriage, and to all the years, adventures, memories, and love still to come.

I love you. Always.

Forever yours,
Kassy ❤️
